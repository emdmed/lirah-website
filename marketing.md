# Lirah Marketing Copy

## Positioning directions

From the persona review (2026-11-08). Reader: `user-persona.md`.

> Lirah gives experienced engineers a structured way to plan, delegate, coordinate and review work done by coding agents. You control the context, the tasks and the merges. Your agents do the implementation.

Headline directions:

1. Control: Put your coding agents to work. Keep control of the work.
2. Category: An engineering workflow for coding agents.
3. Homepage: From engineering plan to reviewed commits.
4. Independence: Orchestrate your agents. Keep your tools.

Messaging territories:

| Territory | Core message |
|---|---|
| Control | You decide what agents see and what they do. |
| Structure | Break ambitious work into tasks you can track. |
| Oversight | Review the work before it lands. |
| Traceability | Know what was done, by which agent, and where it landed. |
| Economics | Measure context and cost per project. |
| Independence | Keep the tools and workflows you already trust. |
